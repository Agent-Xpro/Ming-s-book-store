THE STORY BEHIND THE STORIES

A writer,
before anything else.

Writing has always been a way of paying attention to the world.

To the conversations people have when they think nobody is listening. To the memories that appear at unexpected moments. To the places we leave behind and the people we become along the way.

Every story begins somewhere. Sometimes it begins with a person, sometimes with a question, and sometimes with a single sentence that refuses to disappear.

This website is a home for those stories.
